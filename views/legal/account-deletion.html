Delete your {{ appName }} account

You can ask us to delete your {{ appName }} account and everything linked to it, whether you use the website or the Android app.

How to ask

  1. Email {{ contactEmail }} from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "{{ appName }} account deletion".
  2. We reply to confirm, and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your profile (name, email, student number, exam level), your Google sign-in link if you used one, every paper you sat with its answers and marks, and your inbox messages. Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from short-lived security counters that expire on their own within hours.
