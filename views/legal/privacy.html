Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026 · applies to rivaesa.app and the Rivaesa Android app

{{ appName }} helps students in Botswana practise past examination papers. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Your account: full name, email address, exam level (PSLE, JC, BGCSE or IGCSE) and, if you give it, a student number. Your password is stored only as a one-way scrypt hash.
  • Sign in with Google: if you use it, we receive your name, email address and a Google account identifier. We never see your Google password.
  • Your practice: the papers you start, the answers you choose, your marks and when you sat each paper.
  • Messages: notices from {{ appName }} or your administrator in your inbox.
  • Security data: a sign-in cookie, and short-lived counters of sign-in attempts per email address and IP address to stop password guessing.

What we use it for

Only to run {{ appName }}: signing you in, showing you papers for your level, marking your answers, showing your results, and sending password-reset emails you ask for. We do not show adverts, sell your data, or share it with anyone for marketing.

Who can see it

You can see your own results. {{ appName }} administrators can see student accounts and results so they can manage papers and help students. Our hosting providers store the data for us: Vercel (the website) and Supabase (the database, in the European Union). Google handles Sign in with Google.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When an account is deleted, its profile, answers and results are deleted with it. Security counters expire on their own within hours.

Children

Many of our students are under 18. We collect only what is needed to practise and see results, and nothing is used for advertising. Parents or guardians may contact us to see or delete a child's data.

Your choices

You can correct your name or exam level from your account page, and ask us for a copy of your data or to delete your account.
