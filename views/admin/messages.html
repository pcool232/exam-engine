A few notes

  • Messages show up in a student's Inbox tab immediately -- there's no email involved, so this works even without SMTP configured.
  • "One exam level" reaches every active student currently set to that level (PSLE, JC, BGCSE or IGCSE).
  • This is separate from the automatic welcome message new accounts get, and the automatic notice students get when an exam in their level is published.
