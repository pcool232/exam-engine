Grade 12 Life Sciences - Paper 1
November 2023 - Section A: Multiple Choice

1. Which organelle is responsible for producing most of a cell's ATP?
A. Nucleus
B. Mitochondrion
C. Ribosome
D. Golgi apparatus
Answer: B
Explanation: Mitochondria carry out aerobic respiration, which produces ATP.

2. Which TWO of the following are components of blood? (Choose two)
A. Plasma
B. Chlorophyll
C. Platelets
D. Keratin
Correct Answers: A, C
Explanation: Plasma is the liquid component and platelets help with clotting.

Question 3
During photosynthesis, which gas is absorbed by the leaf?
A) Oxygen
B) Nitrogen
C) Carbon dioxide
D) Hydrogen
Ans: C
Rationale: Carbon dioxide enters through the stomata and is fixed into glucose.

4. The process by which water moves across a semi-permeable membrane
from a dilute to a concentrated solution is called:
(A) Diffusion
(B) Osmosis
(C) Active transport
(D) Phagocytosis
Answer: B
